# TAABEER installation and handover

## Install the theme

1. In WordPress, open **Appearance > Themes > Add New Theme > Upload Theme**.
2. Upload `taabeer-theme-1.0.1.zip`, install it and activate **Taabeer**.
3. Install and activate the free **Elementor Website Builder** plugin.
4. Install and activate **WooCommerce** now if the client needs product management. Purchasing will remain disabled.
5. Open **Appearance > Taabeer Setup** and select **Import Taabeer Demo**.

The importer creates the homepage, collection and Discover Pakistan structure, region drafts, About, Partnerships, Contact, draft legal pages, navigation, Elementor header/footer layouts, editorial taxonomies, a draft Journal story and a hidden draft WooCommerce product. It can be run again without duplicating or overwriting edited page content.

## Review before publication

- Upload the approved logo in **Appearance > Customize > Site Identity**. The included text wordmark is temporary.
- Replace every image under the imported design with client-approved, licensed photography.
- Approve the proposed colour palette, working tagline, regional copy and legal wording.
- Add only verified maker names, locations, techniques, photography credits and product provenance.
- Add the approved public email, telephone, address and social links under **Appearance > Customize > Taabeer Business Details**. Empty fields remain hidden.
- Connect the chosen newsletter service after its consent wording and privacy flow are approved.
- Configure an independent WordPress backup plugin alongside hosting-level daily backups.
- Configure the selected security, anti-spam, SEO and cookie-consent plugins for the live hosting environment.
- Connect Search Console and enter the Google Analytics measurement ID under **Appearance > Taabeer Setup**. Analytics loads only after the visitor accepts analytics cookies.

## Launch WooCommerce

Keep **Enable prices and purchasing** disabled until product information, prices, stock, UK and international shipping zones, tax settings, payment credentials, returns wording and test orders are complete. Then enable purchasing under **Appearance > Taabeer Setup**.

The sample Ajrak product remains a hidden draft with no price. It exists only to demonstrate the editable product structure.

## Elementor editing

The imported pages, TAABEER Header and TAABEER Footer use the free Elementor plugin. Edit layouts under **TAABEER Layouts** in the WordPress admin. Custom TAABEER widgets appear in Elementor for the header, footer, hero, collections, editorial feature, journal grid and contact form.
